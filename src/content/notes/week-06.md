## Accountability for Week 5 Journals

- Nobody submitted Week 5 journal reflections despite having 2 weeks
- Reasons given: school workload, poor time management, confusion about which week was due
- Core challenge: not a time problem but a value problem
  - Everyone admitted having free time in the past 2 weeks
  - “Whatsoever you ascribe worth to, you become creative to create time”

## Time Audit and Discipline

- Busyness is often mismanaged, not genuine scarcity
- Practical test: audit your days and ask where time actually went
  - Calls, gisting, scrolling are common time thieves
  - The enemy’s strategy includes stealing your time
- Structure creates discipline: school works because there are consequences for non-submission
  - Without structure, other priorities get neglected

## Building Consistency Through Accountability

- Question raised: how to stay consistent with goals and habits
- Recommended approach: external accountability with someone you cannot afford to let down
  - Not a lenient friend who will excuse failure
  - Could be a mentor, coach, or parent
- Weekly commitment system:
  1. Send your list of commitments on Sunday
  2. Set a recurring Saturday check-in to defend what you did or did not do
- Financial stakes example: woman gave a friend money with a 90-day deadline to finish her book chapters
  - She finished because the system enforced it
- Brain goes into overdrive when accountability is real; creativity follows

## Meditation: The Practice of Deep Thinking

- Meditation defined as the art of deep thinking, not passive silence
- Three-part structure (based on Pastor Nath’s practice, 15 minutes minimum):
  1. Worship: close eyes, hold a mental picture of God or the throne room
  2. Pray in tongues: charge your spirit man (builds up the inner man, Romans 8:5)
  3. Keep quiet: listen and receive from the Holy Spirit
- Mental picture tip: visualize yourself in the throne room to keep the mind from wandering
- Meditation is repetition: chew a scripture or question 28-32 times, like food needing to be digested
- Think it, say it, act it: confession without meditation does not produce transformation

## Spirit vs. Flesh

- Two-bottle illustration: whichever is fuller (spirit or flesh) rules the individual
- Activities that fill the spirit: reading the word, praying in tongues, hearing sermons, worship
- Proverbs 23:7: “As a man thinketh in his heart, so is he”
  - External life is shaped by what dominates the heart
- Subconsciousness can override the mind of Christ if not renewed
  - Everyone is conditioned from birth; conditioning shapes interpretation of scripture

## Success, Calling, and Hitting the Mark

- Success is not fame, influence, or material gain; it is hitting the mark God set for you
- Kenneth Hagin example: after 15 years of ministry (3 as evangelist, 12 as pastor), God said “well done, now you are entering the first phase of your call”
  - Good works in the wrong assignment do not count as hitting the mark
- Revelation 18 reference: Babylon trades in gold, silver, and “bodies and souls of men”
  - Caution: not everyone who quotes scripture is transacting with Jesus
  - Doctrine of Mammon has infiltrated the church
- Stephanie Ike story: a musician was spiritually contracted in a dream after a gas station encounter; woke to a call confirming it; fled and sought deliverance
  - Illustrates that spiritual transactions are real and the heart must be guarded

## Discerning God’s Will with Neutrality

- Do not outsource your calling to a prophet or external figure
  - A word of prophecy should validate what you have already heard in the secret place
- Entering prayer with neutrality:
  - Acknowledge your preferences and idols honestly before God
  - Lay them on the altar before asking for his will
  - Only then can you receive an unadulterated answer
- Idol redefined: any god, object, or pursuit not directed by God’s revealed will
  - Not just physical objects; includes ambitions, preferences, and conditioned desires
- On-the-go decisions: the mind of Christ is sufficient for daily choices
- Weighty matters: always take time to pray and surrender, because the subconscious can corrupt even Spirit-led reasoning

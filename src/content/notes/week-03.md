## Identity in Christ: Overview

- Identity shapes behavior, speech, appearance, and how we show up
- The word of God is the identity mirror
- Identity grows with everything conquered: babes drink milk, the mature eat strong meat
- The degree to which we walk in identity depends on the depth of our knowledge of God
- Identity sustains faith and controls how we act at each stage of growth

## Oneness with Christ

- Core scripture: Galatians 2:20
  - "I have been crucified with Christ; it is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me"
  - Wherever people see you, they should see Christ
- Also Galatians 4:6-7: because you are sons, God sent the Spirit of His Son into your hearts
  - You take on whatever has been appropriated to Christ: compassion, love, authority, self-restraint
- Practical application: professing identity scriptures daily
  - Example: sticky notes with love scriptures placed in the Bible, confessed each morning
  - "Bimbo, you are patient. Bimbo, you are kind." Appropriating oneness with Christ
- Oneness with Christ is also an invitation to the same intimacy God the Father had with Jesus
  - We approach God as sons, not slaves (prodigal son: came to himself based on who his father was)
- Oneness with Christ gives responsibility, not license for passivity
  - Professing "for me to fail is impossible" without reading is an error

## Positional Advantage

- Core scriptures: Ephesians 1:17-22 and Ephesians 2:6
  - Raised and seated together with Christ, far above principality, power, might, and dominion
  - "Not only in this age but also in that which is to come"
- From that position, you can see things others cannot
  - Illustration: being elevated means nothing below escapes your view
- Personal testimony: praying in tongues, called out a distinct Yoruba name never heard before
  - Later that day, a person with that exact name appeared in a new group chat
  - "That was my very first experience of positional advantage"
- School testimony: a missing exam score reappeared with an A at graduation clearance
  - Never re-registered for the course; the result appeared without explanation
- Positional advantage does not replace diligence
  - The girl who saw exam questions the night before had always studied hard
  - "It doesn't mean you'll be irresponsible and expect your positional advantage to work for you"
- Recommended book: *What Happened from the Cross to the Throne* by E.W. Kenyon
  - Deep read; available in the teens library
  - "I was crying because he explains what Jesus's death and resurrection really means for us"

## Kingdom Responsibilities

- Appropriating identity in Christ is expressed through kingdom responsibilities
- Core scripture: Matthew 6:10, "Your kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven"
  - Kingdom responsibilities are the response to oneness with Christ and positional advantage
- John 17:4: "I have glorified you on the earth. I have finished the work you have given me to do"
  - Our mandate: glorify God and finish the work assigned to us
- Matthew 6:33: seek first the kingdom of God (his will), and all else is added
- The Great Commission (Mark 16:15-18) is threefold:
  1. Preach the gospel of salvation
  2. Bring nations to the knowledge of the truth
  3. Bring territorial and social transformation
- Any vocation can be a vehicle for kingdom responsibility
  - Example: a medical doctor praying "let your healing be dispensed through me to the nations"
  - God then gives wisdom; what you read stays with you

## Intimacy, Gifts, and Growing Capacity

- Mark 3:13-14: Jesus appointed the twelve "that they might be with him" first, then sent them out
  - Being with God qualifies you to be sent and to operate in gifts
  - Intimacy precedes assignment and spiritual gifts
- Matthew 25:14-29 (Parable of the Talents): gifts given according to each person's capacity
  - We grow capacity through prayer, fasting, and reading the Word
  - God will not entrust what will harm you; he gives according to proven stewardship
- On desiring discernment: it is a good desire, but must be paired with doing the work
  - "Covet spiritual gifts" (1 Corinthians 14:1) is a valid pursuit
  - Deja vu-type experiences may be the spirit already operating; build on that through intimacy

## Identity as the Foundation of Purpose

- Purpose cannot function without identity; identity is the lens through which purpose is expressed
- Awakening is structured intentionally: identity first, then purpose
  - "You bring identity to your purpose, to your assignments"
- Chasing purpose without identity leads to a selfish, self-centered lens
- Understanding identity in Christ reframes personality gaps as invitations to grow, not excuses

## Identity Is a Muscle, Not a Destination

- Apostle Paul: "I do not count myself to have attained"
- Even Jesus reminded himself: "Not my will, but yours be done" (he was also fighting)
- Fathers of faith still declare scriptures daily; Bishop Oyedepo quoted as an example
  - Quoting scripture in the car is fighting something in that moment
- Growth markers: things that used to bother you no longer do
  - "There are things that used to bother me before that don't bother me again"
- Constant reminding is not weakness; it is the nature of the flesh warring against the spirit

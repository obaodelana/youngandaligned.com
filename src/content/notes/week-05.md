## Holy Spirit Consciousness

- Central theme: developing an active, daily consciousness of the Holy Spirit as helper and guide
- Relationship analogy: just as a relationship only moves forward when both parties are aware of it, the Holy Spirit’s guidance requires conscious acknowledgment
- Key scripture: John 16, the Spirit of Truth sent to guide into all truth
- Practical posture: wake up and ask, “Holy Spirit, what do you have for me today?”
- Walking in the Spirit requires intentional lifestyle alignment
  - Cannot live carelessly and expect clear spiritual hearing
  - The gifts of the Spirit are a muscle: the more you use them, the stronger they become

## Holy Spirit Listening Exercise

- Blindfold exercise: one person blindfolded, another spotlighted; the blindfolded person asks the Holy Spirit for clues about the spotlighted person
- Purpose: to prove that everyone hears from the Spirit, and to build trust in what you receive
  - Don’t filter what comes; say whatever you hear, see, or feel
  - It often sounds like your own thoughts because the Spirit dwells within you
- Results from the exercise:
  - Eri spotlighted Obaloluwa: sensed emotional rush, need to calm down, make decisions with a level head
  - Obaloluwa spotlighted Bimbo: received “joy and peace,” which resonated accurately
  - Ife spotlighted Halle: received “rest, trust, fix your eyes, Ebenezer,” which resonated deeply given Halle’s current season
  - Esther spotlighted: received “come to me, deep relationship not religion, be more intentional, I love you,” described as “very accurate in a scary way”
  - Koyinsola spotlighted Bimbo: received “I will never leave or forsake you, I am with you always,” and the color blue, which resonated (Bimbo was wearing blue)
- Key takeaway: what you hear is not always audible; it may come as a feeling, a knowing, a word, or a color

## Purpose, Calling, and Assignment: Definitions

- Purpose: the *why* — the intention behind your creation; to glorify God and do His will
  - This is fixed and universal
- Calling: the *how* — how you are uniquely wired to partner with God to fulfill His will
  - Uses your specific design, giftings, talents, and identity
  - Also fixed, but your understanding of it deepens over time
- Assignment: the specific expressions of your calling in a given season
  - These change and can grow as you conquer each one
- Calling and assignments together can reveal God’s purpose for you per season

## Gideon as a Framework

- Gideon’s overarching calling: mighty man of valor, deliverer of Israel
- He partnered with his gifts (leadership, communication, strategy) to fulfill sequential assignments:
  1. Tear down the altar of Baal at his father’s house
  2. Fight the Midianites
  3. Pursue and achieve further victory
- Key insight: Gideon’s self-perception was the bottleneck, not his capacity
  - The angel addressed him directly, yet he responded with “us” six times, deflecting to collective suffering
  - Until he was convicted and the Spirit came upon him, he could not walk fully in his calling
- Vision defined: “Your vision is the sight of your calling that your mind has developed the capacity to carry”
  - As your mind and spirit grow, your capacity to carry the vision expands

## Personality, Gifts, and Taking Stock

- Your personalities, giftings, and capacities are a down payment God has deposited for your destiny
- Practical exercise: sit down and map what you have
  - What traits in your personality do not support your calling? Work to change those
  - What do you need that you don’t yet have? Intentionally develop it
  - What have people said about you since childhood? Write it down; purpose always leaves clues
- Bimbo’s example: childhood nickname was “talking drum”; she later got a communications coach to sharpen that gift for her calling
- Ministry is an assignment or expression, not a calling in itself
  - Calling could be deliverance; expression could be tech, medicine, law, or teaching
  - All believers are called into ministry in some form

## Spirit, Soul, and Body

- Salvation saves the spirit; soul and body are renewed through the spirit over time
- Soul (mind, will, emotions) is the primary battleground: devil attacks soul or body, rarely spirit
- Believer can experience demonic oppression even if spirit is secured
- Everyday life is structured to pull people away from God if they are not intentional

## Speaking in Tongues as Spiritual Technology

- Unexplained sadness can be the spirit picking up danger or God calling deeper
- Speaking in tongues works even when the root cause of distress is unknown
- Pastor Lawrence’s first open vision came after 12 straight hours of tongues
- Described as “charging yourself”: consistent practice transforms mood, cycles, and spiritual sensitivity

## Perspective Exercise (Images)

- Participants viewed a series of images, each revealing a wider context with each reveal
- Lesson: you can only see what your current vantage point allows
- Holy Spirit gives positional advantage, the “bird’s eye view” over life’s circumstances
- Without the Holy Spirit, decisions are made only from what is visible at ground level

## Subconscious Mind Exercise (Airport Visualization)

- Participants imagined packing a bag and traveling to an airport, with no specifics given
- Most defaulted to Uber, economy class, familiar airports
- What the subconscious produced reveals what the belief system has accepted as normal
- “As a man thinks in his heart, so is he”: the body executes what the subconscious believes

## Mindsets and Strongholds

- A stronghold is a sustained, usually faulty thinking pattern fortified in the mind
- Persuasions always come from a reference: environment, exposure, repetition
  - Example: a 7-year-old Black girl in Canada believed her skin was dirty until a trip to Nigeria surrounded her with people who looked like her
  - Example: a friend from secondary school believed all men cheat because she grew up watching it modeled at home
- The abundance of a lie, repeated enough, becomes truth
- Conversely, the repetition of truth is what sets people free

## 2 Corinthians 10:3-5 as the Warfare Framework

- “The weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty in God for pulling down strongholds”
- Three-step operation:
  1. Cast down arguments and every high thing exalted against the knowledge of God
  2. Bring every thought into captivity to the obedience of Christ
  3. Replace the thought: an empty mind invites the stronghold back sevenfold
- The degree of intensity with which a thought is replaced determines the speed of transformation

## Personal Transformation Requires Warfare

- Transformation is not casual: it requires active, daily participation
- Prophecy and calling do not self-execute: you must contend for them
- Shared personal example: wrote a 15-fear list in January 2023 about launching a mentorship platform
  - God responded with Bible verses to counter each fear
  - Took three years to fully own the calling and begin executing
- Gideon example: called himself the least of the least, yet already had the capacity to lead men into battle
- Signs follow every belief, good or bad: self-sabotage follows a belief that one does not deserve good things

## 3 Prayers

- Let me see myself through your eyes
- Let me see who I am in the spirit
- Help me bridge the gap between who I am now and who I am in the spirit

## Christian Faith vs. Self-Belief Without God

- Question raised: what differentiates a Christian’s belief from a non-Christian who also thinks highly of themselves?
- Key distinction: a Christian submitted to God’s will meditates on what God has specifically ordained, not just personal desire
- Principles of God (meditation, belief, confession) work regardless of who applies them, but direction matters
- Non-Christians can achieve worldly success but may be fulfilling a counterfeit version of their destiny
- Being in the world but not of it: the call is to manifest God’s will on earth, not to conform to the world’s definition of success

## The Fatherhood of God

- Core premise: you cannot see above the light within you
  - Eyes guide the body according to the light they perceive; a bad eye misreads the environment
  - Matthew 6:22-23 (light of the body is the eye) grounds this
- God's provision is not conditional on being a Christian
  - Birds of the air neither sow nor reap, yet are fed; humans are of far greater value
  - Even Solomon in all his splendor was not as clothed as the grass of the field
- Jeremiah 29:11 read in context: spoken to captives in Babylon
  - "The thoughts I *think* towards you" is an active, present-tense statement
  - God is always thinking about you; you are valuable and on his mind
- Psalm 139:17: "How precious are your thoughts about me, O God! They cannot be numbered"
  - God's thoughts toward you are too numerous to count
  - Basing your future on your own limited, world-conformed mind is therefore an insult to that provision

## "As Far as Your Eyes Can See": A Correction

- Common misquote attributed to Abraham's story in Genesis 13:14
  - God gave Abraham a *direction*: look northward, southward, eastward, westward
  - "All that you see" followed a directed gaze, not an open-ended personal vision
- "As far as your eyes can see" is New Age framing: based on self, not God's direction
  - Limits God to what the human mind can conceive
  - Abraham's descendants inherited far beyond what his eyes could physically see

## Potential Redefined

- My potential is the **highest thought** God had concerning me when creating me
- Potential is what God sees when He looks at me; it is my most productive state for God's Kingdom agenda
  - God bestowed talents, abilities, and gifts to accomplish those thoughts
  - The question to sit with: *Am I living in my most productive state as regards God's thoughts?*
- Practical exercise: outline every area of life (career, ministry, family, friendships, finances)
  - For each bucket, ask: what does God's most productive version of me look like here?
  - Use scripture to anchor each area, e.g. love: open Galatians 5 and measure against it
- Personal example shared: reviewing the 9-to-5 through this lens surfaced:
  - Not praying for leaders and those in authority (1 Timothy 2:1-4)
  - Not actively praying for colleagues' salvation
  - Not stewarding the workplace as a spiritual territory for intercession
  - Receiving salary without actively acknowledging God as the supply channel
  - Signed up for a $30/month structured organization feeding hungry people, modeled on Jesus' structured distribution of the loaves and fishes

## Vision Boards vs. Seeing What God Has Written

- Psalm 139:16: "In your book they all were written, the days fashioned for me"
  - Your days are already written in the Spirit; plans made outside the Spirit are your own will
- Vision boards done without first pressing into God's presence are based on a conformed mind
  - Better approach: develop capacity to *see* what God has written, then plan from that
- Practical prayer sequence shared:
  1. Thanksgiving and worship, building toward God's presence
  2. Praying in tongues to move beyond the natural mind
  3. Only then: open the journal and write what comes

## Capacity to Hear God

- "Capacity" defined as a *state*, not a skill built over years
  - Hallmark of the state: ability to produce results (hearing, seeing, relating to God)
  - A new believer with a broken and contrite heart can reach the same state as a seasoned one
- Presence is the key marker
  - Moses at the burning bush: "I will now turn aside and see this great sight" (Exodus 3:3)
  - Turning aside is itself an act of capacity
  - You cannot fake presence; results can be faked, presence cannot
- On quick-time decisions (e.g. Daniel asking for one night):
  - Heart posture is the fast-track: heartfelt, fervent prayer (James 5:16)
  - Elijah vs. the prophets of Baal: there was order, structure, and a specific time; protocols matter
- Worship practice that changed everything: worship with an image of Jesus in mind
  - Closes the gap between abstract prayer and actually speaking to someone
  - Makes worship heartfelt rather than emotionally directionless

## Planning, Marriage, and Destiny

- You cannot choose a life partner well based on a short-term, self-derived vision
  - God made Adam walk in his purpose before Eve was brought to him
  - Marrying a version of someone not yet fully developed is unavoidable; the question is whether you grow together in the Spirit
- "I can be anything I want to be" is a doctrine of demons; the reframe is: **I can be anything God wants me to be**
  - Success is measured by God's eyes, not personal ambition
  - What you want has already been conformed to the world by what you have been exposed to

## The Three Stages of Awakening

The journey is structured in three parts:

1. **Discover** — who God created you to be; seeing yourself through God's eyes.
2. **Develop** — cultivating what God has placed within you.
3. **Deploy** — the "fun stage," living out your purpose.

Every person is at one of these stages; movement forward is always possible.

## Hunger as a Differentiator

Hunger is the way we place a demand on our possibilities.

- **Key scripture:** Mark 10:46-52, Blind Bartimaeus.
  - Cried out "all the more" when told to be quiet: increased his hunger to match the resistance.
  - His hunger commanded a response from Jesus.
  - The same people who told him to be quiet were the ones who called him forward.
- **Hunger leads to conviction:** a settled, unshakeable belief that drives action.
- Beware of voices that condition you to be quiet; they often mean well.

## Genesis 1 and the Nature of God

- **"Bara" (Hebrew):** God created the heavens and earth from nothing, no pre-existing material.
  - No baker walks in and says "cake, appear"; God alone creates ex nihilo.
  - If God can create from nothing, no situation is beyond his capacity.
- **Matthew 15:30:** Jesus healed the lame, blind, mute, and maimed (limbs permanently gone).
  - Ties to the power of *bara*: God restores what is completely absent.
- God did not create us for nothing; our existence has intentional, eternal purpose.

## The Power of God and Direction

The power of God flows only in the direction of the Word of God you have received.

- There must be a direction for grace to flow through.
- Blind Bartimaeus was specific: "that I may receive my sight."
- Jesus asked, "What do you want me to do for you?" even when the answer seemed obvious.
- **Tie your goals to a scripture:** find a word that aligns with what you are believing God for.
- **Darkness has a loophole of ignorance.**
  - Without knowledge of who God made you to be, you give darkness room to operate.
  - "My people perish for lack of knowledge"; fear is often ignorance strengthened by darkness.

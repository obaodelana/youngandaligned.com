## Identity Shapes How We Live

- "An identity shift must precede a life shift"
  - Illustration: discovering that you are an heir changes how you see yourself and what you can access
- Identity includes the qualities, beliefs, personality traits, and expressions that make a person distinct
- Identity in Christ means identifying with his nature and who he is
- Recap of the three ways we appropriate identity in Christ:
  1. Oneness with Christ
  2. Positional advantage
  3. Kingdom responsibilities
- Galatians 2:20 and Galatians 4:6-7: the life we live is Christ living in us; we are sons of God
  - Carry the consciousness of oneness with Christ into daily choices
  - Ask: if Christ is living through me, is this what he would be found doing?
- Beliefs sponsor decisions: identity affects behavior, speech, clothing, and how we show up
- Personality is not an excuse for behavior that does not mirror Christ
  - Group example: being prone to lying does not make lying part of your new identity
  - That belongs to the old life, not the life received in Christ
- The Word of God is our identity mirror
  - Read the life of Jesus and examine what in your own life does not reflect him
  - His love and compassion should be expressed through us
- Oneness with Christ feeds both positional advantage and kingdom responsibility
  - The degree to which we walk in kingdom responsibilities depends on our understanding of identity in Christ

## Holding the Word as Authentic and Supreme

- Matthew 8:8: the centurion told Jesus to speak a word, and his servant would be healed
  - He believed the word was true and carried authority; Jesus did not need to come under his roof
- For the Word to profit us, we must hold it as authentic and supreme in every aspect of life
  - Read to understand and apply it, not just to tick a box
- "Faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God"
  - Bimbo shared listening to Galatians during the week and meditating on Paul's description of identity in Christ
  - Listening to the Bible during cooking, cleaning, or commuting creates more opportunities to engage with the Word

## Guarding Identity: The Parable of the Sower

- John 10:10: the thief comes to steal, kill, and destroy
  - Use this lens to recognize what threatens identity, growth, and fruitful relationships
  - Example: offense disrupted a friendship in which praying together had produced results
- Mark 4 shows three ways the Word can be prevented from bearing fruit
- **Access to the heart — Mark 4:15**
  - Satan comes immediately and takes away the Word sown in the heart
  - Illustration: someone who immediately enters a house and takes its gold has gained access to that house
  - Guard your eye gates, ear gates, and mouth; pay attention to what you expose yourself to
- **Lack of roots and endurance — Mark 4:16-17**
  - The Word is received with gladness, but there are no roots; endurance lasts only for a time
  - Tribulation or persecution arises for the Word's sake, and the person stumbles
  - Pressure tests whether we are rooted and grounded in God
  - Examples: compromising convictions because of a relationship or financial pressure
- **The Word being choked — Mark 4:18-19**
  - The cares of the world, deceitfulness of riches, and desires for other things enter in and choke the Word
  - Cares of the world include things competing for time and attention, even ordinary responsibilities such as school
  - The pursuit of money and the desire for more can crowd out spiritual growth
  - When life becomes unnecessarily busy, ask the Holy Spirit to open your eyes and teach you how to prioritize
- Recognizing these patterns helps us examine where our identity is being threatened

## Three Things That Facilitate Growth

- Three things help us grow in identity in Christ: **the Holy Spirit, prayer, and the Word of God**
  - There is no ranking that makes one dispensable; all three are important
  - Declarations and faith-building messages fall under engaging with the Word
- The Holy Spirit helps us guard our hearts, develop roots and endurance, and keep other desires from choking the Word
- It is easy to declare identity in Christ; the Holy Spirit helps us live it when we face pressure
- John 16:7: Jesus promised to send the Helper
- John 16:13-15: the Spirit of truth guides us into all truth, tells us things to come, and glorifies Christ
  - We are not walking blindly or on our own
  - The Helper sent by Christ is the one who helps us live our identity in Christ

## Gideon: From Fear to Deliverance

- Core reading: Judges 6 and 7
- Judges 6:12: the angel greeted Gideon as a "mighty man of valor"
  - Gideon's response was full of questions: if the Lord was with them, why had these things happened?
  - He did not initially recognize the angel of the Lord
- Judges 6:27: Gideon obeyed the instruction to pull down his father's altar, but did it at night
  - He feared his father's household and the men of the city
- Judges 6:34: "The Spirit of the Lord came upon Gideon"
  - He blew the trumpet, and the Abiezrites gathered behind him
  - Bimbo described the Spirit clothing Gideon as wearing him like a glove
- By Judges 7, Gideon was leading Israel into deliverance
  - The Spirit of God changed how he functioned
  - Compare the fearful Gideon at the beginning with the Gideon who acted after the Spirit came upon him

## Acknowledging and Discerning the Holy Spirit

- Develop a relationship with the Holy Spirit through interaction and acknowledgment
  - Rather than dismissing every prompting as "something told me," learn to recognize and thank him for his leading
  - Like a muscle, discernment grows through use
- Bimbo shared a season in which scriptures came to mind during prayer
  - Returning to those passages brought answers to conversations and direction for her life
  - When she thought she had misheard a reference, she kept learning rather than abandoning the practice
- When sharing an impression with someone else, Bimbo often says, "I perceive"
  - Ask the person to take it back to the Word and sit with the Holy Spirit for confirmation
- The Word is the test when you are unsure whether a prompting is from the Holy Spirit
  - Check its consistency with God's will rather than accepting every thought as an instruction
  - Fill yourself with scripture; the Spirit speaks through the Word already in you
- Personal example: the story of Elijah at the brook Cherith came back to Bimbo after a volunteering role ended
  - It helped her recognize that a season had ended and it was time to move on
  - A passage she had previously read became an answer in a present situation
- Mark 3:28-30 was discussed in response to fear about blaspheming the Holy Spirit
  - Bimbo explained the passage in the context of willful blindness and attributing the Spirit's work to Satan
  - Encouragement: study the passage and its context rather than allowing fear to prevent acknowledgment of the Spirit

## Prayer Positions Us for Transformation

- Luke 9:28-29: Jesus went up the mountain to pray; as he prayed, the appearance of his face changed
- Prayer is not doing God a favor; it positions us for growth and develops us
  - Transformation can be happening even when we do not see it physically
  - When tired or reluctant to pray, return to this picture of transformation in the place of prayer
- Prayer, the Word, and the Holy Spirit work together as we grow and discern what we are hearing

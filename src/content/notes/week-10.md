## Opening Reflection: Acts 21 and Interpretation

- Paul warned by disciples "through the Spirit" not to go to Jerusalem
  - They saw correctly (arrest, danger) but interpreted through their own understanding
  - God's will was actually for Paul to go, despite the danger
- Key lesson: we can see or hear correctly but misinterpret
  - "The prophecy was true, but they added a human application which was not of the Lord"
  - Always ask God for interpretation, not just the vision itself
- Agabus also warned Paul dramatically (binding his hands with a belt), yet Paul was unmoved
  - He had already seen God's will for himself directly
- Good intentions can pull us away from God's will
  - Intimacy with God is key to correct interpretation

## Heart Check: Iniquity Found Before It Manifests

- Ezekiel 28:15: "Till iniquity was found in thee" (Lucifer, before the fall)
  - Sin can be present in the heart before it ever manifests outwardly
- Isaiah 14:13: Lucifer said "in thine heart, I will ascend"
- David's prayer: Psalm 139:23-24, "Search me, God, and know my heart"
  - Recommended as a consistent personal prayer: remove every offensive way
- Personal testimony shared: convicted by the Holy Spirit after a sharp phone call with a contractor
  - Did not insult, but made the person feel less of themselves
  - Holy Spirit surfaced it later that night: "Love is patient, love is kind"
  - Had to repent, cry, and surrender; felt a release and a step into a new measure
- Warning: when offense starts appearing frequently, guard your gates
  - "When a new level is coming, watch out for offense"

## Extended Bible Reading: Building Faith and Power

- Testimony shared: woman assigned to finish the New Testament in one week
  - Chose to fast three days and read it twice as punishment for missing the deadline
  - Sat and read through the night; finished by noon the next day
  - Fruit: father had a dream and wanted to attend church; house caught fire but was unharmed; uncle received 11 years of back pay from a company he never knew he worked for
- Power is generated through extended, continuous reading of Scripture
  - Reading one chapter with deep meditation is good, but breadth also builds faith
  - Challenge: read 10 chapters a day, or read whole books in one sitting (e.g. Romans, 1 Corinthians)

## Definition of Ministry

- A minister is one whose life is motivated by their love for Jesus, with the goal of seeing Jesus revealed and glorified
  - Ministry is defined not by the activity, but by the motivation and the goal
  - "Ministry is first from your heart and an overflow of your secret place"
- Ministry is about meeting needs, solving problems, and providing solutions
  - No true ministry is self-serving
- Examples of false motivation:
  - Doing good deeds for public recognition
  - Evangelizing to accumulate "crowns" or blessings
  - Giving to church to receive material returns
- 2 Chronicles 25:1-2: Amaziah "did what was right in the eyes of the Lord, but not wholeheartedly"
  - God cares about the heart behind the action, not just the action itself
- Every participant is a minister: the 9-to-5, healthcare, teaching, friendship all qualify
  - As long as the motivation is love for Jesus and the goal is to glorify him

## Kingdom Ambassadors

- An ambassador represents a government greater than themselves
  - Each believer is heaven's ambassador
- The representation of ambassadorial duties is expressed through ministry
  - Being an ambassador without the consciousness of ministry means operating without the right motivation
- Micah 4:1-4: the mountain of the Lord becomes the standard; nations flow to it for wisdom and justice
  - The church (mountain of the Lord) is meant to feed into and influence every other sphere

## Mountains of Influence

- Seven mountains framework (developed 1975, rooted in Micah 4)
  - Each mountain represents a sphere that shapes human culture, values, and civilization
  - All seven exist for one purpose: thy kingdom come, glorifying God
- The seven mountains:
  1. Religion: spirituality, faith, fivefold ministry
  2. Family: foundational unit of society; devil actively fighting this mountain
  3. Education: transfers and preserves knowledge structurally
  4. Arts and Entertainment: shapes the idea of success; eye gate, ear gate, atmosphere
  5. Media: shapes perception; where ideas are sold (songs, news, social media)
  6. Politics/Government: controls policy formation; believers must participate or become victims of policy
  7. Business and Finance: funds every other mountain; marketplace, corporations
- Two additional mountains not in the original seven:
  - Science and Technology (pseudo-eighth mountain): includes healthcare
  - Healthcare fits here because it is ultimately driven by financial systems
- Mountains can have sub-mountains (e.g. family psychology, fashion under arts)
- Believers are sent to their mountains as ministers and ambassadors, not just to "hammer" (succeed materially)

## Calling vs. Assignment

- Calling: the overarching, governing purpose over your life on earth
- Assignment: your specific role within a mountain of influence
  - You can have multiple assignments across different mountains
  - Example: assignments in religion, education, business, and media can all flow from one overarching calling
- Ephesians 2:10: "We are God's workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works"
  - Those good works = God's will on earth as it is in heaven
- The revelation of the need and will of God is the basis for all kingdom advancement
  - Intimacy with the Holy Spirit is required to know that will
